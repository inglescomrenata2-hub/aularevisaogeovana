Part 2

Speaking Time 🗣️

Speaking 1 · Warm-up (2 min)
Introduce yourself as if you're meeting a new client for the first time. Include your name, what you do, where you're from, and one thing you love about your work.

💡 Language to use

  • My name is…
  • I'm a businesswoman / entrepreneur
  • I have a beauty business
  • I'm quite passionate about…
  • I'm based in…
2:00
✏️ What to listen for Check: correct use of "I'm", "I have", vocabulary from Class 1. Did she use any of the new adjectives? Note any tense or vocabulary mistakes gently after she finishes.
Speaking 2 · Past Tenses (2 min)
Talk about how your business started. What did you do? What was happening in your life at that time? What changed?

💡 Language to use

  • I opened my salon in…
  • At that time, I was studying…
  • I used to… / I didn't use to…
  • While I was working, I…
  • Things changed when…
2:00
✏️ What to listen for Check: past simple vs. past continuous; correct use of "used to"; irregular verbs (opened, started, had, went). Did she use "while" correctly?
Speaking 3 · Roleplay 💅🏼
Let's role play! You are a nail technician talking to an English-speaking client. I'll be the client.
📋 Situation
A tourist from the US walks into your salon and asks for a nail appointment. She doesn't speak Portuguese. You need to welcome her, ask what she wants, describe the options, and tell her the price and wait time.

💡 Useful phrases

  • Welcome! How can I help you?
  • Would you like…?
  • We have… / We offer…
  • It might take about… minutes
  • The price is quite reasonable
  • Is that okay for you?
3:00
✏️ What to listen for This is free production — celebrate any correct use of "might", "quite", "would you like". Notice vocabulary gaps and note them to address in next class. Keep tone encouraging throughout the roleplay.
Speaking 4 · Quick Fire Round ⚡ (no repeating!)
Answer each question in at least 2 sentences. You have 30 seconds each. No repeating words you already used!
💬 What did you use to do on weekends before you had the salon?
💬 Describe your ideal client using 3 adjectives.
💬 What might you do differently in your business next year?
💬 Are you getting used to anything new in your life right now?
